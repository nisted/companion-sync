Privacy policy

Companion Sync. Last updated 14 October 2026.

The short version

Companion Sync runs on your own computers. It copies your Bitfocus Companion config between them through the storage you choose. Nothing is sent to the developer, and nothing is collected, tracked or shared.

What it accesses

  • Your Companion config on each of your rigs, so it can be copied between them.
  • The storage you choose, signed in with your own account:
    • Google Drive: only the files and folders Companion Sync creates itself (the drive.file permission). It cannot see any other files in your Drive.
    • GitHub (limited): only the one repository you choose to give it.
    • GitHub (quick): permission to create and use your repositories, so it can make a private one for the config. It only ever reads or writes that one repository.
    • Dropbox, OneDrive: these services grant access to your files, but Companion Sync only reads and writes its own CompanionSync folder.
    • Network folder or iCloud Drive: only the folder you pick.

Where your data goes

  • Your config is stored only in your own storage account, and on your own rigs.
  • Sign-in tokens are kept on each of your computers and are only used to reach your own storage.
  • The developer never receives your config, your sign-in details or any information about how you use the app. There are no analytics.

Google user data

Companion Sync's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google Drive is used only to store and retrieve your own Companion config, and is not transferred to anyone else.

Removing access

You can remove Companion Sync's access at any time from your Google, GitHub, Dropbox or Microsoft account settings. Deleting the app and its CompanionSync folder removes everything it stored.
